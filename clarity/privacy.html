Clarity

Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Clarity is a skincare tracking app operated by Marshall Zafiriou ("we"). This policy explains what Clarity collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Clarity is a cosmetic progress tool; it does not diagnose or treat medical conditions.

Information Clarity collects

  • Skin profile. Answers you give during onboarding, such as skin type, sensitivity, goals, concerns, product preferences, and whether you are pregnant or breastfeeding (used only to avoid recommending unsuitable ingredients).
  • Scan measurements. Scores and observations produced from your guided face scans, including breakout activity, marks, texture, region readings, and the locations of possible breakout clusters.
  • Routine and product activity. Your routine, daily check-ins, completed steps, product searches, barcode lookups, and products you view.
  • Account identifier. Clarity creates an anonymous account identifier when you first use it. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number to use the app.
  • Subscription status. Whether you have an active Clarity Pro subscription and its renewal state.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider records standard request logs (for example IP address, time, and request type) for security and reliability.

Face photos

Photos you take for a scan are uploaded over an encrypted connection so they can be analyzed, and are deleted from our servers after analysis. Copies used for your before-and-after comparisons are stored only on your device. Clarity does not use face recognition to identify you.

How information is used

  • To measure visible skin characteristics and show your progress over time.
  • To build your routine and rank products by how well their ingredients fit your profile.
  • To provide subscriptions, restore purchases, and keep the service secure and working.

Scans are never used to improve our models unless you explicitly opt in from Settings. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. Clarity contains no third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Service providers

We share information only with providers that operate the service on our behalf, under their own security and privacy terms:

  • Render hosts the Clarity servers and database.
  • Cloud object storage holds scan photos temporarily during analysis.
  • Apple processes subscription payments. We never receive your payment card details.
  • RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription status linked to your anonymous identifier so Clarity can unlock paid features.
  • Product databases (such as Barcode Lookup and Open Beauty Facts) receive the barcode you scan, and nothing else, to identify a product.

Retailer links open the retailer's own site, which is governed by that retailer's privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Delete individual scans at any time with Settings → Privacy → Manage & delete scans.
  • Export the data held on our servers with Settings → Privacy → Export my data.
  • Delete your account with Settings → Privacy → Delete my account and data. This permanently removes your server records and the scan history and photos Clarity stores on your device.
  • Turn Allow model improvement on or off in Settings → Privacy. It is off unless you turn it on.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete your data. Contact us at support@clarityskin.app and we will respond within 30 days.

Retention and security

We keep account data while your account exists and delete it when you delete your account. Information is encrypted in transit, and access to production systems is restricted.

Children

Clarity is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used Clarity, contact us and we will delete the information.

Changes

We will update the effective date above when this policy changes and highlight material changes in the app.
