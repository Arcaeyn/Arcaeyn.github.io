Restore a purchase

Open the paywall or Settings → Subscription and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple ID you subscribed with.

Cancel your subscription

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions → Clarity. Subscribers can also tap Manage subscription in Clarity's Settings.

Delete your account and data

In Clarity, open Settings (the person icon on the Products tab), go to Privacy, and choose Delete my account and data. This permanently removes your server records and the scans and photos stored on your device.

Getting a reliable scan

  • Face a window or soft, even light, and avoid strong shadows or colored lighting.
  • Remove makeup if you can, pull hair back, and wipe your camera lens.
  • Scan at the same time of day in the same spot so week-to-week comparisons are fair.

Medical concerns

Clarity tracks cosmetic changes only. For painful, spreading, or worsening skin, contact a dermatologist or other qualified clinician.
